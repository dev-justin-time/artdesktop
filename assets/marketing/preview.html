05 / Original art direction

Superseded explorations.

These AI art explorations set the visual tone but used claims that were not true for the build. They are kept for reference, not for launch.

Do not ship the originals. They claim “WebGPU”, “zero latency”, “10 creative engines”, “v1.0 launch edition” and “download free”. The current build has no WebGPU renderer, no published installer and no measured zero-latency path.
