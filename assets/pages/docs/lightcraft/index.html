LightCraft

Fast, private RAW editing and color grading.

LightCraft is a fast, private RAW photo editor with light, color, tone curve, masking, presets and black-and-white tools for cinematic color grading.

Open in the suite Release notes Agent access

Features, one by one

What LightCraft does in raw photo editing, documented feature by feature.

01

RAW processing

Edit RAW captures on your own machine without uploading originals.

02

Light adjustments

Shape exposure, contrast and highlights in the light workspace.

03

Color grading

Grade color with cinematic controls and a dedicated color workspace.

04

Tone curve

Adjust luminosity and contrast precisely with an editable tone curve.

05

Masking

Apply local adjustments through masks that follow the subject.

06

Presets and profiles

Reuse looks with presets, profiles and the color mixer.

07

Crop and geometry

Crop, straighten and correct perspective and lens geometry.

08

Black and white

Convert color photographs to black and white with tonal control.

09

Local and private

Process photos natively, with no account and no cloud round-trip.

FAQ

Does LightCraft run locally?
Yes. LightCraft is a native, private editor: photos are processed on your machine, and the suite bundles it for offline use.
What editing controls does LightCraft offer?
LightCraft covers light, color, effects, tone curves, masking, presets, profiles, the color mixer, crop and geometry, and black-and-white conversion.
