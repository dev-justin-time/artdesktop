ArtCraft Journal - Volume 04

Field Notes on Generative Form

Words by A. Rivera · 8 min read · March 2026

The first sketches were not drawings at all. They were instructions: a set of constraints, a palette, and a rule for what happens when two shapes disagree. The canvas did the rest.

Working this way changes the rhythm of a studio. Instead of placing every anchor point by hand, you tune the system and watch it answer. The interesting moment is not the first render but the third or fourth, when the system starts to surprise you.

The shape of a signal

A signal is a promise that something will change. In motion work we spend most of our time shaping that promise: easing it, delaying it, or breaking it on purpose.

Design the constraint, not the artifact. The artifact is only one sample of the system.

Fig. 01 - Study in cyan and violet, generated from a four-node graph.

When the tool and the material share a vocabulary, iteration becomes conversation. That is the quiet argument for a single, tightly coupled studio: every module can speak about the same asset without translation.
